I started my career building software, then moved into architecture and engineering leadership.
Over more than 20 years, the scale of the work changed: I went from designing systems and leading teams of 10 to leading organizations of more than 80 people across onshore and offshore groups.

As my responsibilities grew, so did the complexity of the systems and organizations I supported.
Much of my work has focused on modernizing critical platforms without disrupting the businesses that depend on them.
That work taught me how to move forward without sacrificing stability: meet today’s needs, improve the underlying system, and leave less technical debt behind.

Today, I apply that experience to production AI and agentic systems.
I still lead like a developer and architect: close enough to understand the work, ask useful questions, and help teams decide when to move quickly, when to protect reliability, and when technical ambition creates real business value.
