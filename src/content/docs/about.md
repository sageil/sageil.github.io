I lead technology organizations that must improve delivery while modernizing the systems beneath it.
Across more than two decades, I have worked as a developer, architect, manager, and director.

That technical foundation shapes how I lead.
I stay close enough to architecture and delivery details to challenge assumptions, guide decisions, and help teams balance pace with reliability, risk, technical debt, and measurable value.

My aim is to create an operating environment where teams have clear ownership, decisions are transparent, and reliable delivery becomes repeatable.
The organization and its systems should become more capable with every cycle of work.

I created [KodaCode](https://github.com/sageil/kodacode), an open-source terminal coding agent, as a practical way to study and improve AI-assisted software delivery.
It is one part of my broader work on engineering systems and trustworthy automation.

My view of AI is pragmatic: “Humans steer, agents execute.”
People remain responsible for intent, standards, and judgment.
Agents can take on structured investigation and execution when the work has clear boundaries and verification.
